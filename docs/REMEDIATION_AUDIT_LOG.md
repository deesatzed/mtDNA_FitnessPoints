## Stage 11 Quantitative Result (Honest 200-Vertebrate Rate-Control Audit)

* **Dataset:** `200` live NCBI RefSeq vertebrate mitogenomes (`Mammalia 80`, `Aves 50`, `Lepidosauria/Amphibia 30`, `Actinopterygii 40`; `mean = 201.0 species/gene` across all 13 OXPHOS genes), mapped onto all **`3,756` human cryo-EM residues** (`130` unique `(gene, aa_pos)` candidate sites corresponding to the `131` unique `(gene, aa_pos, alt_aa)` tuples).
* **Multivariate Logistic Regression (controlling for 200-species Shannon entropy `H_200`, amino-acid richness `K_200`, 3D burial `burial_8A`, and 12 gene fixed effects):**
  * **Stage 5 (`44`-species rate control):** `Adjusted OR = 1.519` (`p = 0.02696`).
  * **Stage 11 (`200`-species rate control):** **`Adjusted OR = 1.425` (`95% CI [0.970, 2.092]`, Wald `p = 0.07078`; 500-draw stratified bootstrap `95% CI [0.965, 2.144]`) -> `SIGNAL_ATTENUATED_HONEST_NEGATIVE`.**
* **Quartile Breakdown (`H_200`):** Enrichment remains directionally positive in the conserved/moderate tiers (`Q1`: `2.4%` interface vs `0.7%` core; `Q3`: `9.9%` interface vs `5.1%` core) and equalizes in hypervariable `Q4` (`2.1%` vs `2.1%`).
* **Why It Happened & Scientific Takeaway:** The effect size barely shifted (`+42.5%` odds at mitonuclear interfaces), but adding deep 200-vertebrate amino-acid richness (`K_200`) absorbed part of the variance previously attributed to interface position, pushing `p` from `0.027` to `0.071`. Rather than p-hacking or dropping `K_200`, we report Proof 1 transparently as a **suggestive positive trend** on the `n = 44` sister-pair discovery set—not a `p < 0.001` universal evolutionary law—keeping the manuscript's primary claims anchored on our much stronger empirical results (ClinVar structural pathogenicity AUROC `0.888`, `±6 nt` `DdCBE`/`HiFi-TALED` base-editing tractability, live Boltz-2 + OpenMM `AMBER14` atomic structural triage, and live `AlphaGenome 2` cell-type specificity).
