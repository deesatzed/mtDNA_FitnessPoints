### Core Biophysical Principle: The "Two-Cliff" Fitness-Beats-Truth (`FBT`) Trade-Off

1. **Microsecond Matrix Geometry:** Superoxide ($\mathrm{O}_2^{\bullet-}$) is generated *inside* the inner mitochondrial matrix (Complex I $I_Q$ site during Reverse Electron Transport `RET`, and Complex III $Q_o$ site) and reacts with iron-sulfur clusters and cardiolipin in microseconds. Matrix **`SOD2`** dismutates $\mathrm{O}_2^{\bullet-}$ at near diffusion limit ($k \approx 2 \times 10^9\ \mathrm{M}^{-1}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$, `~10^6×` faster than Vitamin C), while **`UCP2`**, **`NDI1`**, and **`AOX`** prevent the reduced ubiquinone ($Q_{\text{red}}$) back-pressure from creating $\mathrm{O}_2^{\bullet-}$ in the first place.
2. **Why Single-Gene `COX3 W116R` Alone Hits Cliff #2 (Conditional `E3/E4` Kinetic Hypothesis):** Scott et al. (*PNAS* 2011) measured **higher affinity for reduced cytochrome *c* (lower $K_m^{\text{cyt }c}$) and lower $V_{\max}$** in bar-headed goose Complex IV (the $\mathrm{O}_2$ binuclear center sits in `COX1`, >20 Å away). Modeling this as an **effective lumped intracellular hypoxic-flux shift** (`Km_app: 1.25 → 0.53 kPa`, conditional phase-diagram hypothesis) shows that saving `ATP` during severe hypoxia (Cliff #1) in an un-adapted human nuclear background amplifies succinate-`RET` superoxide bursts upon re-oxygenation (Cliff #2). An intact bar-headed goose heart avoids Cliff #2 because its **capillary/sarcolemmal micro-architecture + avian nuclear subunits + native antioxidant/uncoupling capacity co-evolved** over millions of years.
3. **Positive Epistasis Solution (`Driver + Matched Buffer`):** Pairing **`COX3 W116R` + `Human SOD2`** (`S = 1.385×`, `95.1%` LHS positive synergy, **100% human protein sequence, zero foreign immunogenicity**) or **`+ Yeast NDI1` (`1.322×`) / `+ Ciona AOX` (`+4.8 kPa` cliff widening)** clears *both* cliffs simultaneously.

## 4. Spatial Architecture: Organ-/Compartment-Focused vs. Global Systemic Delivery

### Why Global Systemic Adult `mtDNA` Engineering Is Currently Infeasible
* **Copy-Number & Organ-Mass Barrier:** Rewriting `≥85%` of `mtDNA` across trillions of cells (`brain + heart + kidneys + 30 kg skeletal muscle`) *in vivo* is far beyond current `AAV` or `LNP` biodistribution (hepatic sink traps `>80%` of standard IV LNPs; blood-brain barrier excludes large cargoes; systemic high-dose viral vectors trigger fatal hepatotoxicity/complement activation).
* **Stoichiometric Reality Check:** Even global editing cannot violate conservation of oxygen mass (`P/O` ratio `~5–6 ATP` per $\mathrm{O}_2$ molecule)—in end-stage mechanical lung destruction (`IPF`/`COPD`), mitochondrial optimization lowers tissue $Pa\mathrm{O}_2$ tolerance and trims `10–15%` proton-leak waste, but cannot manufacture missing oxygen molecules across scarred alveoli.
