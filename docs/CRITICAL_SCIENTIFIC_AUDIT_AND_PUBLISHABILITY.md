## 3. Five Hardest Reviewer #2 Critiques of Our Own Prior Work (Stages 0–10)

### Critique #1 (CRITICAL — Bioenergetic Accuracy): Mis-Anchoring Bar-Headed Goose `COX3 W116R` Kinetics (`Scott et al., PNAS 2011`)
* **What our code currently implies:** In `stage4_epistasis_synergy_uq.py` (line 57) and `stage7a`/`stage8a`, we parameterized `COX3 W116R` as `km_o2_mult = 0.42` (`Km_O2: 1.25 → 0.53 kPa`) and cited *Scott et al., PNAS 2011* as the benchmark.
* **What Scott et al. (*PNAS* 2011) ACTUALLY measured:** Bar-headed goose cardiac cytochrome *c* oxidase showed **higher affinity for *reduced cytochrome c* (lower $K_m^{\text{cyt }c}$) coupled with a *lower* $V_{\max}$**—NOT a `2.4×` drop in intrinsic Complex IV oxygen-binding affinity ($K_m(\mathrm{O}_2)$)! Moreover, intrinsic isolated Complex IV $K_m(\mathrm{O}_2)$ is already `~0.05–0.15 kPa` (`0.4–1.0 mmHg`), and the $\mathrm{O}_2$-binding heme $a_3$–$\mathrm{Cu}_B$ binuclear center sits inside **`COX1`**, `>20 Å` away from `COX3 W116`.
* **Reviewer #2 Impact:** **Instant desk rejection or major revision** if claimed as an experimental `Km_O2` fact!
* **Exact Fix:** Reframe `km_o2_mult` explicitly as an **effective lumped intracellular apparent hypoxic-flux parameter (`E3/E4` hypothesis)** capturing altered cytochrome *c* redox kinetics / proton-channel coupling under hypoxia, cite *Scott et al. (PNAS 2011)* accurately (`altered cyt c affinity & lower Vmax`), and never claim Scott measured `Km_O2 = 0.53 kPa`.

### Critique #2 (CRITICAL — Mathematical Circularity): Hand-Assigned Driver Parameters in Stages 4, 8B & 10B ODEs
* **The flaw:** In Stage 4 (`COX3 W116R`: `km_o2_mult=0.42, ret_sensitivity=1.48`), Stage 8B (`ND4L Y85H`: `heat_leak_resist=0.42`; `COX1 I57V`: `cold_fluidity=0.34`; `ATP6 I106V`: `baro_resist=0.44`), and Stage 10B, **the allele-specific kinetic perturbations were encoded by hand from qualitative evolutionary archetypes**, not computed from first-principles quantum/MD free energies or measured in wet-lab kinetics.
* **Reviewer #2 Impact:** A quantitative reviewer will say: *"Your `N=10,000` LHS only varies global parameters around hand-typed allele effects—so proving `ND4L Y85H` wins at `41.5 °C` while `UCP2` hurts is circular with your own RHS equations!"*
* **Exact Fix:** **Separate the paper cleanly into two distinct claims:**
  1. **Empirical Discovery (`E1/E2/E3` — Zero Circularity):** Comparative genomics + cryo-EM interface enrichment + blind OOS ClinVar AUROC `0.888` + `±6 nt` zero-bystander `TALED`/`DdCBE` screening (`6/131`) + live **Boltz-2** (`10` structures) + live **OpenMM `AMBER14`** (`COX3 W116R` 6-bond trident vs. `ATP6 L156R` `11.4×` distortion; `5/5` thermal/baric leads `STABLE`) + live **AlphaGenome 2** (`41.0` vs. `3.0` SNVs, `p = 0.0357`; `UCP2` cardiac switch; `SOD2` `Lung AEC2` peak). **None of this is circular!**
  2. **Conditional Dynamical Phase-Diagram Analysis (`E3/E4` — Explicitly Framed as Parametric Requirements):** Frame Stages 4/7A/8A/8B/10B NOT as *"proving `ND4L Y85H` saves `0.36` viability"*, but as **deriving the exact falsifiable kinetic boundary conditions** (*"IF an evolutionary allele produces an effective hypoxic-flux shift $\Delta K_{\text{app}}$ with coupled $\Delta\Psi$ liability $\gamma_{\text{RET}}$, THEN what minimum buffer strength, heteroplasmy `≥85%`, glycolytic unmasking (`Trap B`), and pre-op lead time (`-12 h to -18 h`) are mathematically required to clear both cliffs?"*). That framing is **100% rigorous and bulletproof** in *PLOS Computational Biology* / *eLife*!

### Critique #3 (HIGH — Evolutionary Genomics Power): `44` Species & Borderline `p = 0.027` Without Codon-Level Phylogenetic Models
* **The flaw:** Our Stage 1–2 scan used `44` wild vertebrate mitogenomes (`23` sister pairs) with entropy-stratified permutations and logistic regression (`Adjusted OR = 1.519, p = 0.02696`, matched control null `OR = 1.194, p = 0.136`). While clean and honest, `44` species is small by 2026 comparative-genomics standards, and `p = 0.027` sits right near the `0.05` threshold without formal tree-aware codon models (`HyPhy aBSREL/MEME/RELAX`, `PAML branch-site`, or `PCOC` profile convergence).
* **Reviewer #2 Impact:** Evolutionary biologists (*MBE*, *Genome Biology*) will demand `150–300+` vertebrate mitogenomes + phylogenetic generalized least squares (`PGLS`) / `HyPhy` to confirm whether `OR ≈ 1.5` strengthens (`p < 0.001`) or washes out at scale.

### Critique #4 (HIGH — Scope Sprawl vs. Depth): Too Many Unvalidated Indications Diluting the Core Discovery
* **The flaw:** Jumping from human cryo-EM & `TALED` editing to 24 clinical indications (24-week micro-preemies, hospice air hunger, blast hearing loss, burns, `CO`/cyanide, `COPD`/`IPF`) + honeybees + corals + Atlantic salmon in a single paper triggers **"kitchen-sink overreach"** alarm bells for journal editors!
* **Exact Fix:** **Keep the 24-indication Master Plan as an internal translational strategy deck**, and **focus Paper 1 ruthlessly on ONE crisp, airtight story**: *MitoFitness Atlas — Structural, Base-Editing (`±6 nt`), Atomic (`Boltz-2` + `OpenMM`), and Single-Cell Regulatory (`AlphaGenome 2`) Prioritization of Extremophile mtDNA Adaptations & Compensatory Human Buffers (with `Ex Vivo` CAR-T / Organ-Perfusion as the focused prospective testbed).* Keep Tier-3 Ecological Conservation (`Stage 10B`) as a **separate** companion note or link to `Mito_Papers` Paper III.

### Critique #5 (MEDIUM — Structural & Base-Editing Blind Spots)
* **Missing in-silico checks a structural/editing referee will ask for:**
  1. **Real-world `TALED` genome-wide nuclear & mtDNA off-target risk:** `TALED` (`TadA8e` + split `DddA`) has documented **transcriptome-wide RNA editing** and **mtDNA bystander/off-target A→G deamination** outside the `±6 nt` spacer window (Cho et al., *Cell* 2022 / 2024 `V28R`/`R111P` high-fidelity `TALED` variants!). We should explicitly specify **HiFi-`TALED` (`V28R` / `sTALED`)** and deep duplex sequencing (`≥10,000×`) in our wet-lab SOP.
  2. **Explicit lipid-bilayer MD (`ns–μs` timescale):** Our OpenMM `AMBER14 + OBC2` is implicit-solvent local energy minimization (`L-BFGS`), **not** explicit POPC/cardiolipin membrane molecular dynamics (`100 ns` NPT).
