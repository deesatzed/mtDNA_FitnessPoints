Plain-English Guide to the MitoFitness Atlas (College Freshman Level)

New to mitochondrial genomics or biophysics? Here is what every tab means in everyday language (full printable document in docs/EXPLORER_GUIDE.md and physician brief in docs/PHYSICIAN_EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.md).

The Big Idea in 3 Sentences

Wild extremophile animals (bar-headed geese flying over Everest, deep-diving whales, naked mole-rats) evolved tiny single-letter upgrades in their mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) to survive low oxygen. This platform tests which wild upgrades physically fit inside human 3D mitochondrial proteins without causing human disease, can be written cleanly with modern base editors, and what happens during an acute oxygen crash + sudden oxygen rush (like a heart attack or stroke).

Why a Single Upgrade Isn't Enough ("The Two-Cliff Trap")

Installing the goose COX3 W116R upgrade rescues cellular energy (ATP) during oxygen starvation, but when oxygen rushes back in, the revved-up engine causes a worse toxic free-radical (ROS) spike. Pairing it with a protective nuclear buffer (Human SOD2, Yeast NDI1, or Ciona AOX) clears both cliffs—turning 1 + 1 into > 2 (positive epistasis).
